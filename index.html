MEET BRIA

hello

Hi, I’m Bria, the mom behind Homeschool FTS.

I homeschool two kids at very different stages of learning, which has taught me quickly that there is no single “right” way to homeschool. What works for one child, one subject, or even one season of life may not work for the next. For me, homeschooling is about having the flexibility to pay attention, adjust, and build an education around the child instead of forcing the child to fit into a rigid system.

I created Homeschool FTS to share the real side of our journey: the planning, the lessons, the resources, the wins, the things that flop, and everything we learn along the way. I want this to be a practical place where families can find ideas they can actually use without feeling like their homeschool has to look perfect.

AT THE HEART OF EVERYTHING HERE

Freedom.The freedom to create an education that fits your family.
Tools.The tools to make that education possible.
Success.The confidence to define success in a way that makes sense for your child.

I’m learning right alongside my kids, building Homeschool FTS as we go, and sharing what genuinely works in our home. If something we discover makes another family’s homeschool journey a little easier, then this space is doing exactly what I hoped it would do.

Welcome to Homeschool FTS. I’m glad you’re here.
