The 3:50 AM Emails Count

September 17, 2026 · Mom Life

In the last 48 hours I created an admissions portal, verified a tutoring account, registered for a course enrollment system, and signed up for an unschooling workbook series.

I also sent my 11th grader her weekly work email at 3:50 AM because that's when I finally had a quiet minute to pull it together.

This is not because I'm an overachiever. It's because I'm a mom who needs every option to stay open while I figure out what actually works.

Some of these registrations are backup plans. Some are resources I want to try. Some are just me being thorough because I've learned that “we'll figure it out later” usually means “panic at midnight the night before the deadline.”

The beautiful part is that while I'm doing all this invisible admin, I also got my digital planner store working end-to-end. Stripe checkout. Cloudflare secure download. Two dollars a planner. Real products on a real site.

The less beautiful part is that I did it on three hours of sleep while my household accounts pinged me about expired cards and a domain renewal I can't afford to lose.

If you're homeschooling and you're also juggling enrollment systems, tutoring codes, and the quiet terror of wondering if you're doing enough, you're not alone.

The 3:50 AM emails count. The portal logins count. The “just in case” registrations count.

None of it is glamorous. All of it is keeping the thing alive.
